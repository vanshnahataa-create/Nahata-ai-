Allow camera for gestures?

Nahata.AI will use your front camera to track your hand, so you can zoom, move and rotate the core without touching the screen. The video is analysed on your device only. It isn't recorded, saved or sent to the server. The hand-tracking model is downloaded from Google and jsDelivr when you switch this on. Your browser will also ask for camera permission, and the CAM button turns it off anytime.

STARTING
THUMB + INDEX: pinch or spread to zoom
THUMB + INDEX + MIDDLE: move hand to spin, turn hand to roll

Voices

Pick the most fitting voice installed on this device for each mode. Deep British male voices work best. Lower depth sounds deeper.

Allow phone commands?
Nahata.AI will be able to open YouTube, Maps, directions, Google or the dialer when you ask, by voice or text. It can't see your contacts, messages, photos or files, and it can't change your phone's settings or turn it off. These commands are handled inside this page and aren't sent to the server. Calls always need a tap to confirm, and the PHONE button switches this off anytime.
